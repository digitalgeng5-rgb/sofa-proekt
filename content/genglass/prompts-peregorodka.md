# Промты для изображений: комната со стеклянной перегородкой GENGLASS

Перегородка по фактам из knowledge-base/brands-gen-group.md: тонкий стальной профиль,
матовая порошковая покраска (RAL 9005 или 9016), закалённое стекло. Алюминий не упоминать.

## Вариант 1. Насыщенный интерьер (без минимализма)

```
Interior photography of a cozy, richly furnished living room in a Moscow apartment, divided by a floor-to-ceiling glass partition with a slim black powder-coated steel frame (matte RAL 9005), clear tempered glass with a band of fluted glass at the bottom, a glass sliding door and a transom above. Behind the glass, a bedroom with an upholstered headboard and soft bedside lamps is visible. In the foreground: herringbone oak parquet, a large vintage Persian rug, a deep green velvet sofa with many cushions and a knitted throw, a carved wooden coffee table with books and a ceramic vase, a tall bookcase filled with books and objects, framed paintings in a gallery wall, ceiling moldings and wall panels, a brass floor lamp, linen curtains, large indoor plants in pots. Warm afternoon daylight from a tall window passes through the glass partition into the bedroom, soft shadows, realistic reflections on the glass. Eclectic, layered, lived-in interior, rich textures and warm colors. Shot on full-frame camera, 24mm lens, eye level, natural light, photorealistic, high detail, 3:2
```

Негатив: `minimalism, empty room, white walls only, sterile, scandinavian minimal, aluminum frame, thick frame, distorted glass, warped lines, extra doors, people, text, watermark, cartoon, CGI look, oversaturated`

Замены стиля: неоклассика / ар-деко / тёплый лофт (см. историю чата v1, при необходимости дописать).
